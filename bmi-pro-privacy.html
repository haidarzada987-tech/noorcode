Privacy Policy — BMI Pro

Last updated: 2 November 2026

BMI Pro is a body-mass-index (BMI) and weight-tracking app developed by Noorullah Haidari. This policy explains, in plain language, how the app handles your information. The short version: the app works entirely on your device and collects nothing.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following information locally on your phone or tablet only, so the app can do its job:

  • Profiles you create (name, gender, age, height, current weight and target weight)
  • Your weight history records
  • App settings (language, theme, measurement unit, reminder on/off)

This data never leaves your device on its own. It is not sent to the developer or to any server, because the app has no server and no accounts.

What the app does NOT do

  • No sign-up or account of any kind
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • No collection of contacts, photos, location, or files
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with anyone

Backup export and import

Settings includes an optional backup feature. If you choose “Export backup”, the app creates a file containing your profiles and weight history and hands it to the share sheet of your device, so you decide where it goes (for example your own cloud drive or a message to yourself). The developer never receives this file. “Import backup” reads a backup file that you pick and restores it on your device.

Notifications

If you turn on the weekly weigh-in reminder in Settings, the app asks for the notification permission and shows one local reminder per week (Fridays at 09:00). The reminder is created and delivered entirely on your device; no data is sent anywhere for it. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Children

The app is a general health calculator and tracker. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this page will be updated and the “Last updated” date above will change.
